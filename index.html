Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move between plans at any time from workspace settings; changes apply to the next billing period.

What counts as an assistant prompt?

Each request you send to the Bloom assistant, such as “summarise these notes”, counts as one prompt.

Is there a discount for non-profits or education?

In a real launch we’d offer one. In this concept preview, every plan is free to explore.

Do I need a card to start?

No. The Seed plan is free, and this preview never asks for payment details.
